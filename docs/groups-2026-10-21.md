# Groups inside a family (#107)

*Assessment and plan, 2026-10-21. **DECIDED 2026-10-22** — D1 a (the creator is admin), D2 a (the owner cannot see a
group they are not in), D3 a (the family owner's assistant switches apply), D4 **b** (transcripts as in a direct
chat: the sender's consent alone). Everything else is as recommended below. docs/protocol.md has the `### Groups`
section and the amendments listed in §6.*

The issue: *"Family members can organise groups inside the Family. Groups visible only for members. AI also should
work in these groups."* The owner added: groups can be created and deleted, and **the family chat must be untouched**.

## 1. What a group is

A chat of kind **`group`**, inside one family, with a **name** and an explicit **member list**: some of the family's
members, never anyone outside it. It is the family chat with fewer people — the same bubbles, sender names, threads,
polls, stickers, voice and video messages, formulas and diagrams — plus a name and a member list somebody manages.

- **Visible only to its members.** A group a member is not in is not in their `GET /chats`, sends them no frames or
  pushes, and every endpoint answers them `chat_not_found` (404) — the same as a chat that does not exist, so its id
  cannot be probed. (Today a chat you are not in answers `not_chat_member` 403; for a group that would confirm it
  exists.)
- **The family chat is untouched.** It stays the one chat everybody is in, created with the family, never renamed
  through this, never deleted, its rules unchanged. Nothing about a group reaches it, and nothing in it reaches a
  group.
- **Who can be in one:** current members of the same family. At least **two** people (the creator and one other); a
  group of two is allowed, because a group with the assistant and a name is not the same thing as a direct chat.
- **No calls** in a group: the protocol has no group calls (`invalid_call`, as in the family chat today).
- **No "seen" ticks**, as in the family chat: many readers. Typing is shown, as in the family chat.

## 2. Managing a group — **D1**

| Action | Decided (D1 a) |
|---|---|
| Create (name + members) | any family member |
| Rename | the group's **admin** |
| Add members | the admin |
| Remove a member | the admin |
| Leave | any member |
| Delete the group (and all its messages, for everyone) | the admin |

- The **admin** is the creator. When the admin leaves the group, leaves the family or deletes their account, the
  member who joined the group earliest becomes admin (the family-owner hand-off rule, applied to the group). When the
  last member leaves, the group is deleted.
- **Deleting** asks first ("Delete "<name>"? Its messages are deleted for everyone.") and is final: the group, its
  messages, threads, polls, reactions and the attachments only it referenced go. Members' clients drop it at once.
- Alternatives: **(b)** every member may add, remove and rename; only the admin deletes. **(c)** no admin: every member
  may do everything, deleting included.

## 3. The family owner and groups — **D2**

**Decided (D2 a): the owner has no power over a group they are not in, and cannot see it.** That is what "visible
only for members" means, and it matches direct chats, which the owner cannot see either. What the owner keeps is what
they already have everywhere: reports (a member may report a message in a group, and the client says the owner will
see it, as for a direct chat), and removing a member from the family, which removes them from every group.

Alternative **(b)**: the owner sees the list of the family's groups (names and member counts, never messages) and may
delete one.

## 4. The assistant in a group — **D3**

It works as it does in the family chat: a message that starts with `@ai` (or picks "Ask the Assistant" from the
paperclip, or the assistant at the top of the `@` list) is answered in the group, for everyone in the group to read.
The asker's consent is required, as everywhere.

- **What it reads:** that group's own recent messages (30 days, 200 messages, 40 000 characters), filtered by each
  sender's consent — never the family chat, never another group, never a direct chat or a private thread. A mention in
  the family chat never reads a group either.
- **The switches — decided (D3 a): the family owner's switches apply to groups exactly as to the family chat** —
  Sees recent history (`ai_history`), Photos (`ai_vision`), Recent photos (`ai_history_photos`), Faces (`ai_faces`),
  Lookups (`ai_lookups`). They are the family's policy on what may leave the server, and one set is simpler to explain
  than one per group. The owner's Assistant settings say "the family chat and groups".
  Alternative **(b)**: each group's admin sets its own history/photos switches, starting off.
- **Language:** the family's language, as in the family chat.
- **Its prompt** says it is in a group chat named "<name>" of some of the family's members, and that it sees only this
  group.
- **The daily greeting** stays in the family chat only.

### Transcripts of somebody else's recording — **D4: decided (b)**

- **As in a direct chat: that sender's consent alone.** The owner's `ai_transcripts` is about the family chat and does
  not reach a group (the owner chose this over following D3).
- (Not chosen: (a) as in the family chat — the owner's switch AND the sender's consent.)

Your own recordings: your own consent, as everywhere. The consent screen's direct-chat line becomes "In a direct
chat or a group, the others in it can ask for the text of your voice notes, audio files and videos…", and every
consent and owner-settings line about what a mention sends says "the family chat or a group" (the transcripts
switch alone keeps "the family chat").

## 5. Everything else, decided by the protocol as it stands

- **Member mentions** work in a group, and only the group's members can be named (the server refuses anyone else with
  `validation`). The `@` list offers the assistant first, then the group's members.
- **Polls** work in a group; the assistant's "4 of 5 voted" counts the group's members.
- **Blocking** is the family chat's model: a blocked member's messages are hidden rows on the blocker's side and still
  delivered; no push. Adding someone to a group is never refused because of a block, in either direction — a refusal
  would tell the adder about a block they are not party to.
- **Leaving the family** (or being removed) removes the member from all their groups, with the admin hand-off above.
  Rejoining does **not** put them back: a group is by invitation, and a member who rejoins is added again by its admin.
- **Deleting an account:** the member's messages in a group stay, under "Deleted account", as in the family chat; they
  leave every group, with the hand-off.
- **Family statistics** already count every chat in the family; a group's messages count the same way.
- **Push titles:** `"<Group> — <Sender>"`, and `"<Group> — <Sender> mentioned you"`, in the device's language.
- **Reporting the assistant** covers its answers in a group too (the operator reads the report).
- **Retention, threads, reactions, edits, stickers, rich blocks, voice and video messages:** unchanged, they already
  work in any chat the member is in.

## 6. On the wire

- **`Chat`** gains, for `kind: "group"` only: `title` is the group's name; **`member_ids`** (array, everyone in it,
  the caller included); **`admin_user_id`**. `peer_user_id` stays null. Old clients ignore the new fields.
- **`POST /chats/groups`** `{title, member_ids}` → `201 {chat}`. The caller is added and becomes admin.
  `validation` for a title empty or over 64 characters (after trimming) or no other member; `not_same_family` for a
  member outside the family.
- **`PATCH /chats/{id}`** `{title}` → `200 {chat}` — admin only.
- **`POST /chats/{id}/members`** `{user_ids}` → `200 {chat}` — admin only.
- **`DELETE /chats/{id}/members/{user_id}`** → `200 {chat}` — the admin removing someone, or anyone with their own id
  (leaving). Leaving answers `204`.
- **`DELETE /chats/{id}`** → `204` — admin only. Never the family chat (`validation`), never a direct or `ai` chat.
- A new code **`not_group_admin`** (403) for a member who is not the admin; non-members get `chat_not_found` (404).
- **Two new frames:** `chat_updated` `{chat}` to every member when a group is created, renamed or its members change
  (so a member just added sees it at once), and `chat_removed` `{chat_id}` to a member who left or was removed, and to
  everyone when the group is deleted. Old clients ignore unknown frames; `GET /chats` stays the reconciler.
- **Limits:** a title up to 64 characters; up to 50 groups per family.
- **Old clients** (before this ships) show a group as a plain chat with its name and can read and send text in it, but
  offer no mentions, polls or `@ai` list; iOS and Android builds from before this keep a deleted group on the device
  until they update. That is acceptable for a family that updates its apps — the alternative, hiding groups from old
  clients, needs a capability handshake the protocol does not have.

### Server
- Migration `0056_groups`: `kind` CHECK gains `group`; a group's shape is both pair columns NULL; new columns
  `chats.title` (groups only) and `chats.admin_user_id` (FK, `ON DELETE SET NULL`) — **not** `user_a_id`, because
  deleting an account deletes every chat whose `user_a_id` is that account. `assistant_reports.chat_kind` CHECK gains
  `group`. `chat_members` already holds explicit rows for every kind.
- Every branch on kind (the server map lists 17): mentions, `model_surface`, polls, titles, push wording, transcript
  `allowed()`, reports, the assistant's prompt, `ensure_chat_access` (404 for non-members of a group), and
  `remove_membership` / account deletion (the admin hand-off and the empty-group delete).

### Clients (all six)
- The chat list: groups below the family chat, ordered by their last message like any chat, with a group avatar (the
  initials of the name in a circle, or a "people" glyph).
- **New group**: in the "New Chat" sheet (iPhone/iPad, Android) and a new button in the chat list header (Mac, web,
  Windows): a name and the members to add.
- **Group info**: the name, the members, and the admin's Rename / Add / Remove / Delete; Leave for everyone.
- Every "family chat only" check becomes "family chat or group" where §4–5 say so: sender names, mentions (with the
  group's members as candidates), polls, `@ai`, the video button, consent notices, the transcript rule, notification
  titles. The web's "anything else is a direct chat" fallback goes.
- iOS and Android drop any chat that leaves `GET /chats` (today only direct chats), and every client handles
  `chat_updated` / `chat_removed`.
- Every new string in all nine languages.
